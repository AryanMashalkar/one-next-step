# Demo video narration script

Read at a calm pace. Each paragraph matches one scene of the video (about 95 seconds total).

**Intro.** Meet Maya. She's a student with ADHD. When she opens a to-do list with ten things on it, she doesn't pick one. She freezes. This is One Next Step, built for exactly that moment.

**Dump.** First, Maya dumps everything on her mind, one thing per line. No sorting, no priorities, no pressure. Then she tells the app how much energy she has, and how long she can focus.

**Start.** Instead of the whole list, she sees one thing. A small, concrete action she can start in under two minutes. And it tells her why this comes first. It noticed the landlord email is due today.

**Done.** When she's done, she presses D. The next step of the same task appears, so she keeps her momentum.

**Split.** If a step still feels too big, she presses S, and it shrinks. Just get set up. Then two minutes. Starting is the hardest part, so that's where the app helps most.

**Skip.** Not ready for this one? N moves it to later. No guilt, no red overdue badges.

**Timer.** A calm focus timer runs while she works, and the full list stays hidden, unless she chooses to peek at it.

**A11y.** Everything works from the keyboard. Every new step is announced to screen readers through a live region, which you can see in the top corner. It can read the step aloud, and it respects dark mode and reduced motion.

**Finish.** Step by step, the list gets done. And at the end, she sees what she achieved, not what's left. Nothing is sent to a server. It's one static page that works offline and keeps her progress on her own device.

**Outro.** One Next Step. Not a better list. Just the next thing to do.
